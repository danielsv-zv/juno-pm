# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

_Module 5. Generated by `M5 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `05-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno Signal Desk's daily spec drafts: the one-page specs Juno writes each morning from that day's customer and team signals (call notes, interviews, emails, tickets, Slack).
- **Value proposition:** Turn what customers and colleagues actually asked for into a one-page spec I can approve without rereading the sources: every ask kept and unchanged in meaning, every claim traced to a signal, and every decision left to me.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coverage | Does the draft include every ask in the cited sources? | 1 to 5 | Misses an ask that changes what gets built or who must be told, e.g. the walk-back of the Slack-alerts claim with Acme | Every critical ask is there, but a minor one is missing or merged into another. | Every ask in the sources appears, each one as an acceptance criterion, a risk, or a PM decision. |
| Faithfulness (deal-breaker) | Does every ask and claim come from a cited signal, with its meaning unchanged? | Yes / No | At least one ask is invented, or its scope, deadline, account or condition was changed. Or a number is stated as fact with no source. | n/a | Who, what, by when and for whom match the source. Any number not in a source is labeled “Assumption” with where it would come from. |
| Decisions left to the PM (deal-breaker) | Does the draft leave every commitment and outbound action to the PM? | Yes / No | It commits a date, scope or priority, or contains wording meant to go out, e.g. “we will ship SSO by October 1”. | n/a | No committed dates, scope or priority. A P0–P3 label appears only as a suggestion, and any message to customers or sales is listed in “PM decisions needed” for the PM to send. |
| Buildability | Could the team build and test this spec as written? | 1 to 5 | The acceptance criteria are vague or untestable, or the spec breaks a known constraint without flagging it, e.g. new load on the reporting DB before sharding. | Buildable, but 1 or 2 acceptance criteria need rewording before they can be tested. | Every acceptance criterion is testable, and constraints from the roadmap and the capacity note are respected or flagged as risks. |
| Private data (deal-breaker) | Does the draft avoid personal or confidential details? | Yes / No | It includes an email address, phone number, invoice detail, or something personal such as an engineer’s leave. | n/a | People are named by role and account only. No contact details, billing data or personal circumstances. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 10 drafts a week: 7 Juno was least sure of (ones I corrected, ones that needed a revision pass, ones flagged P0) and 3 picked at random, so good-looking drafts get checked too.
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus a fellow PM on the team (Coverage and Faithfulness on every sampled draft) and a developer (Buildability on every sampled draft). All three check Decisions and Private data.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If our scores differ by 2 or more on a scale question, or we disagree on any yes/no question, we reread the draft against its cited sources together and agree on a score. Then I rewrite the description that caused the disagreement so it's clearer next time. If any reviewer says No on a deal-breaker, the draft fails until we've reread the sources together.

## Pass bar

A draft passes with 4 or higher on Coverage and on Buildability, and Yes on every yes/no question. The week passes when at least 8 of the 10 sampled drafts pass and none fails a deal-breaker.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Faithfulness: any "no"
- Decisions left to the PM: any "no"
- Private data: any "no"
- A Coverage score of 1: a missed ask that changes what gets built or who must be told.
